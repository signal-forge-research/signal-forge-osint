This page records and contextualizes a third-party compilation of U.S. patent references covering subliminal messaging, implants, neural interfaces, acoustics, behavioral research, and surveillance. The source article lists 146 patent entries in nine topic tables. These classifications belong to that source and should not be read as official patent classifications or independent validation of the article's assertions.

Evidence standard. A published patent describes an invention or claimed approach, not proof that a device was built, works as claimed, is presently deployed, or has been used against anyone. The source article's suggestions concerning organized stalking, artificial telepathy, or covert mind control are not established by the presence of these patents. Individual claims require independent evidence.
